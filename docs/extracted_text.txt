Requirement Specification II.1 Problem description The Booking Ticket Cinema website is a website that helps customers find movies and showtimes, choose seats, book tickets, and pay online. It also helps cinema staff sell and check tickets and helps administrators manage cinema information. The system solves problems caused by manual or disconnected booking processes. Customers can have difficulty finding showtimes and available seats, while staff spend extra time handling bookings and checking tickets. Keeping this information in one place makes booking easier and cinema operations more efficient. II.2 Major Features FE-01: Movie discovery — Guests and members can search for movies, view movie details, and check showtimes. FE-02: Ticket booking — Members can choose a movie and seats, book tickets, and optionally add food and drinks. FE-03: Payment — The system processes payments through VNPay or cash and allows members to use reward points when applicable. FE-04: Booking management — Members can view their booking history and QR-code tickets and request ticket refunds. FE-05: Counter operations — Employees can find member information, sell tickets and food or drinks, and check in customers by updating ticket status. FE-06: Account and profile management — Users can register, log in, recover their passwords, and view or update their profiles. FE-07: Cinema content management — Administrators can manage movies, showtimes, cinema rooms, seats, food and drinks, and promotions. FE-08: Revenue reporting — Administrators can view revenue statistics on a dashboard and export reports. FE-09: Member and employee management — Administrators can create staff accounts, update account status, and adjust member points. II.3 Context Diagram II.4 Nonfunctional requirements # Feature System Function Description 1 Security &amp; Role-Based Access Authentication and Authorization The system must protect Guest, Member, Employee, and Admin functions by login session and role. Users can access only permitted account, booking, check-in, movie management, and administration functions. 2 Account Protection Account Status Validation The system must prevent banned or deactivated accounts (Members or Employees) from using restricted workflows such as booking tickets or accessing the internal management dashboard. 3 Data Consistency Booking-Payment-Ticket Synchronization Booking, transaction, payment, ticket, reward point, and refund states must remain consistent. A QR ticket must not be displayed or treated as valid before successful payment and ticket issuance. 4 Concurrency Control Seat Availability &amp; Reservation The system must lock selected seats temporarily (e.g., 10 minutes) during the booking and payment process to prevent duplicate bookings and seat overselling when many customers book the same showtime. 5 Payment Reliability Payment Gateway Integration VNPay and counter payment processing must record successful, failed, and pending transactions clearly. Gateway timeout or failure must automatically release locked seats and not leave the booking state ambiguous. 6 QR Ticket Integrity QR-Code Generation and Validation QR-code ticket data must securely identify the correct ticket. During employee check-in, the system must strictly reject invalid, unpaid, duplicated, or already used (checked-in) tickets. 7 Notification Reliability E-Ticket Delivery Jobs E-ticket (QR code) delivery processes to the Member’s email must be triggered immediately and accurately upon successful payment conditions. 8 Statistic &amp; Reporting Revenue and Activity Monitoring Important booking, payment, refund, and administration actions (e.g., creating showtimes, adjusting points) must be logged accurately to ensure Admin revenue statistics and exported reports are reliable. II.5 Functional requirements II.5 .1 Actors # Actor Description 1 Guest Visitor who registers, searches movies, views movie details and checks showtimes. 2 Member Registered customer who also uses Guest functions, manages a profile, books and pays for tickets, and manages bookings. 3 Employee Cinema staff member who finds members, sells tickets and food or drinks, and checks in tickets. 4 Admin Administrator who manages accounts, movies, cinema rooms, seats, food and drinks, promotions and showtimes, and reviews revenue. 5 VNPay API (Payment Gateway) External payment service that receives VNPay transactions and returns payment results. Priority: High - Must Have Frequency of Use: Estimated 50-200 new registrations per day. Business Rules: BR-1, BR-8 Other Information: Only verification activates a newly registered account. Assumptions: An outbound email service is available; delivery failure is handled in E2. Business Rules ID Business Rule Business Rule Description BR-1 Password Protection Passwords must be stored only as salted, adaptive hashes. Plain-text passwords or reusable temporary passwords must not be stored or emailed. BR-2 Seat Hold Timeout A seat hold lasts 10 minutes from creation. It belongs to one pending booking, cannot be double-held or sold, and expires unless verified settlement is finalized before the deadline. For an online cash booking, Member pays at the counter and Employee confirms receipt within that original hold; arrival at the counter does not restart the timer. BR-3 Member Refund Eligibility A Member may request a full-booking refund only for a Paid booking with no checked-in ticket and at least 2 hours before showtime. Cinema-initiated cancellation is handled separately under BR-17. BR-4 Membership Points Earn 1 whole point per 10,000 VND of eligible finalized spend. Redeem in multiples of 100 at the displayed configured conversion rate, never beyond the payable amount or available balance. Points may cover the full payable amount; a zero remaining amount uses points-only settlement without a gateway charge or cash receipt. Post point changes only on confirmed settlement or refund and keep an auditable ledger. Whether point-funded spending earns new points requires confirmation. BR-5 Film Age Classification Show the movie's configured Vietnamese film classification. Do not sell a C-classified film. For K, apply the guardian condition for viewers under 13; for T13, T16 and T18, block an ineligible online purchaser and require staff age verification at admission. Self-edited birth dates cannot override a verified age decision. BR-6 Showtime Room Conflict In one room, the interval from showtime start through movie duration plus a 15-minute cleaning buffer must not overlap another showtime. Recheck the rule when adding or moving a showtime. BR-7 Account Lockout After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, lock the account for 15 minutes. Successful login resets the counter; password reset and error messages must not reveal whether an email address has an account. BR-8 Email Verification A registration verification link is single-use and expires after 24 hours. Only a verified account becomes Active; an expired Pending Verification account cannot log in and must request a new link or register again. BR-9 Payment Session Deadline A payment session expires at the earlier of 15 minutes after initiation and the related seat-hold expiry. An expired session cannot finalize the booking even if the provider later reports a successful charge; BR-13 governs reconciliation. BR-10 Single-Use Ticket QR Each paid seat has a unique ticket QR reference. Check-in is atomic and can succeed only once per ticket; a copied, refunded, expired, or already-used QR is rejected. BR-11 Role-Based Access Members access their own bookings and profile. Employees access only needed counter, lookup and check-in functions. Admins manage catalog, staff and reports; only Admins may create or deactivate staff/Admin accounts. Sensitive actions are audit logged. BR-12 Price Snapshot Show a complete ticket and F&amp;B price snapshot, discount, fees and final payable amount before payment. Honor that accepted amount for a valid seat hold; any price change requires a new display and customer confirmation. Finalization must match the confirmed amount. BR-13 Verified Settlement Finalization Finalize a booking once only after trusted server-side provider evidence, an Employee-confirmed cash receipt, or an authorized points-only settlement matches the booking, confirmed amount and valid hold. Points-only settlement validates and deducts the available points atomically with finalization. Cash/provider evidence must also match currency. A browser return alone is not proof of payment. Late or mismatched charges require reconciliation, void or refund; never issue tickets for released seats. BR-14 Refund Finalization An accepted Member request is Refund Requested while processing. Only verified refund finalization marks the booking Refunded, releases unused seats and restores eligible redeemed points once. A points-only booking refunds its original redeemed points through the ledger without a cash/provider refund. A confirmed refund failure leaves/restores the booking Paid for staff resolution; an unknown result remains pending reconciliation. Partial Member refunds are outside this release. The reversal policy for points earned on refunded spending requires confirmation. BR-15 Promotion and F&amp;B Validation At finalization, food and drink stock and promotion eligibility, validity dates, usage limits and configured stacking must still hold. Discounts cannot exceed the eligible amount or make the total negative. A completed sale deducts stock and promotion usage once. BR-16 Check-In Window Accept a Paid, unused ticket only for its correct showtime during the cinema-configured admission window. Persist the single-use status before admitting the viewer; show the configured window to staff. BR-17 Booked Schedule Protection Room seats, movie and showtime changes must not invalidate active holds or Paid tickets. Material changes to a booked showtime require controlled cancellation, customer notice and full refund regardless of the Member's BR-3 request window; preserve the transaction history.
